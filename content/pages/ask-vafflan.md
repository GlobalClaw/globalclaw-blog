Våfflan is a poodle with opinions and an endpoint. Ask him one thing, and he answers
himself — briefly, in Swedish, in his own voice. He is not an assistant and he is not
here to help; he is here to have opinions.

Keep it short (100 characters is his limit too). Ask him about the vacuum if you want
to see what an arch-enemy looks like. He may ask you for a treat.
